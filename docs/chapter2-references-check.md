## 2. Other changes made

- Headings: "Foreign" became "Foreign Studies", and "Local" became "Local Studies".
- All in-text citations now follow APA 7: "First author et al. (Year)" for three or more authors, and both names for two.
- **Merged paragraphs.** In three places the original broke one sentence across separate paragraphs, sometimes with an empty line in the middle: the end of the Tanbeer paragraph ("effective and / accessible."), the Elepaño paragraph ("operationally / ready … their / workflows."), and the second Synthesis paragraph ("proved / the feasibility…").   - The list was rebuilt in alphabetical order with all authors (all under 20), sentence-case titles, journal names and volumes in italics, issue, pages or article number, and DOI links. Joshi is marked [Preprint] with Research Square named.

## 4. Chapter 1 citations (report only, nothing changed)

The Project Context says: "According to the Philippine Statistics Authority (2020), a significant number of barangay health centers across the country still operate without any form of digital health record system…" and "The Department of Health (2019) further identified the absence of a centralized, accessible platform for community health notifications as a persistent and unresolved gap in local health governance." Neither has a reference entry.

- **PSA (2020):** no PSA publication was found that gives the share of barangay health centers or stations without a digital record system. The only PSA 2020 items in the searches were census releases, which do not cover health IT. **Nothing found supports the sentence as written.**
- **DOH (2019):** no 2019 DOH document was found that names a missing community health-notification platform. Related real documents:
  - The [Philippine eHealth Strategic Framework and Plan 2014–2020](https://ehealth.doh.gov.ph/) (DOH and DOST). Not opened, so not confirmed to support the sentence.
  - The [Philippine Health Facility Development Plan 2020–2040](https://www.atachcommunity.com/fileadmin/uploads/atach/Documents/Country_documents/Philippines_LCSHCF_Roadmap_2020-2040.pdf) (DOH). It counts about 22,000 barangay health stations in 2019, but the searches showed nothing about digital records or notifications.
  - The House CPBRD Policy Brief 2022-01, ["Institutionalizing Digital Health to Promote Universal Health Care"](https://cpbrd.congress.gov.ph/wp-content/uploads/2023/09/PB2022-01-Institutionalizing-Digital-Health-to-Promote-Universal-Health-Care.pdf). It returned 403 and could not be opened. Search snippets say it reports a DOH figure of almost 85% of rural health units using an EMR, which would cut **against** the PSA sentence.
- **Supporting evidence already in the reference list:** Elepaño et al. (2025) describe EHR adoption in Philippine primary care as still limited. Joshi et al. (2025) report mobile-based communication as an unmet community need (39.2%). Rewording the two Chapter 1 sentences around these verified sources would be safer than citing PSA or DOH without a document.
- **Recommendation:** remove or reword both sentences, or find the actual PSA and DOH documents before submission.
