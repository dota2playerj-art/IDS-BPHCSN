Security
{#- WAS four items across: "Encrypted Connection" (wifi), "Role-Based Access" (key), "Secure Auth" (shield) and "Trusted Gov System" (seal). Three are gone, for three different reasons. "Encrypted Connection" was false. This application is served by app.run() over plain HTTP at 127.0.0.1:5001 -- no ssl_context in app.py, no TLS setting in config.py -- so the password typed into the field directly above this panel travels in cleartext. It stated the opposite of what happens, on the screen where the claim matters most. "Secure Auth" named no mechanism, so there was nothing a reader could go and check. What is real here is narrower than the label: passwords are stored as werkzeug hashes and never in plaintext, and a deactivated account is refused even when the password is right. Both are about storage and about who is let in -- not about the sign-in being secure. Against that, SECRET_KEY falls back to a literal in config.py, so the session cookie this form issues is signed with a value anyone reading this repository already has. "Secure" was not a thing the system had earned the right to assert. "Trusted Gov System" claimed an accreditation. Nothing in this codebase, its schema or its documentation records a licence, an endorsement or a registration of any kind. There was no such designation to point at. "Role-Based Access" stays, alone, because it is the one a reader can verify: every route behind this form carries @role_required, and an account that reaches a screen its role does not admit is redirected away rather than served. #}
